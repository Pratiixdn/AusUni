Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 2024

1. Information We Collect

AusUni collects minimal personal data. We may collect:

  • Session data: Anonymous session identifiers used to remember your bookmarks during your visit.
  • Usage data: Pages visited, search queries, and general navigation patterns (anonymised).
  • Contact form data: Name and email address if you contact us via our contact form.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use collected information to:

  • Provide and improve the AusUni service
  • Respond to contact form inquiries
  • Analyse aggregate usage patterns to improve content

We do not sell, trade, or share your personal data with third parties for marketing purposes.

3. Cookies

AusUni uses cookies for:

  • Session cookies: To maintain your bookmarks during your visit.
  • Theme preferences: To remember your light/dark mode preference.
  • Analytics: We may use anonymised analytics to understand site usage.

4. Third-Party Links

AusUni contains links to official university websites and external resources. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of these external sites. We encourage you to review the privacy policy of any site you visit.

5. Advertising

AusUni may display advertisements provided by third-party advertising networks, including Google AdSense. These networks may use cookies to serve ads based on your browsing history. For more information, visit Google's Ad Settings at adssettings.google.com.

6. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Request access to any personal data we hold about you
  • Request deletion of your personal data
  • Opt out of analytics tracking by using browser privacy settings

7. Contact

For any privacy-related queries, contact us at: our contact page.
