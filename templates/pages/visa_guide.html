Australian Student Visa (Subclass 500) Guide

Complete step-by-step documentation checklist and process categorized by Assessment Levels (AL1, AL2, and AL3).

1. Understanding the Process

The Australian Student Visa (Subclass 500) allows you to live, work, and study in Australia for up to five years. The process generally involves:

  1. Apply to an Australian Educational Institution: Receive a Letter of Offer.
  2. Accept your Offer: Pay tuition fees and receive an Electronic Confirmation of Enrolment (eCoE).
  3. Gather Documents: Based on your Assessment Level (Country and Provider Risk).
  4. Lodge Visa Application: Apply online via ImmiAccount.
  5. Biometrics and Health Check: Complete examinations if requested.
  6. Visa Decision: Wait for processing and receive your visa grant notice.

2. Document Requirements by Priority Level

Australia assesses visa risk based on a combined country and education provider rating. This defines whether you need to provide regular evidentiary documents (AL1) or more rigorous proof (AL2 & AL3).

Priority Level 1 (Low Risk / Streamlined)

Applicants from Low-Risk countries or those applying to highly-rated universities face a streamlined process. You must declare you have sufficient funds and English proficiency, but documentary evidence is usually not required upfront.

  • Identity Documents: Valid Passport, National ID, Birth Certificate.
  • eCoE: Electronic Confirmation of Enrolment from your university.
  • OSHC: Proof of Overseas Student Health Cover.
  • GTE Statement: Genuine Temporary Entrant statement explaining why you are studying in Australia.
  • Note: The Department of Home Affairs can still ask for financial or English proof during processing if they see fit.

Priority Level 2 (Medium Risk)

Applicants falling under Level 2 must provide concrete evidence of English proficiency and sometimes financial capacity upfront.

  • All Level 1 Documents
  • English Language Proficiency: Valid IELTS, PTE, or TOEFL scores meeting the minimum requirement (typically IELTS 5.5 to 6.5 depending on the course).
  • Financial Evidence (May be requested): Proof of funds to cover 12 months of living costs (approx. AUD 24,505), tuition fees, and travel costs.
  • Academic Transcripts: Certified copies of your previous educational qualifications.

Priority Level 3 (High Risk)

Applicants from High-Risk countries must undergo a rigorous evidentiary process. Strict scrutiny is applied to financial documents, GTE, and academic history.

  • All Level 1 & 2 Documents
  • Strict Financial Evidence (Mandatory):
    • Bank statements covering the last 3-6 months.
    • Evidence of income of the person funding you (parents or spouse) showing an annual income of at least AUD 25,000 (or AUD 35,000 if accompanied by family)
    • Educational loans from approved financial institutions.
  • Comprehensive GTE/GS (Genuine Student) Profile: Detailed evidence of ties to your home country (job offers, family, property) to prove you will return after studying.
  • Employment History: Resumes, payslips, and reference letters from previous employers to cover any study gaps.
  • Relationship Evidence: If bringing dependents, birth certificates and marriage certificates are strictly verified.

Important Disclaimer

Visa regulations and financial requirements are frequently updated by the Australian Department of Home Affairs. This guide is for informational purposes only. Always consult the official Department of Home Affairs website or a registered MARA agent before lodging your application.
