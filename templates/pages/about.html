About AusUni

Your independent guide to studying in Australia

AusUni was built for one reason: to make it easier for international students to find honest, clear, and detailed information about Australian universities — all in one place, completely free.

Our Story

AusUni started with a simple frustration: finding reliable, up-to-date information about Australian universities as an international student is surprisingly hard. University websites are complex and difficult to compare. Tuition fees, English requirements, scholarship deadlines, and application processes are scattered across dozens of different pages and portals.

We built AusUni to solve that problem. Our goal was to create a single, easy-to-use platform where any student — whether in Nepal, India, the Philippines, Nigeria, or anywhere else in the world — could come to understand their options and make informed decisions about studying in Australia.

Today, AusUni covers over 40 Australian universities across all states and territories, with detailed information on courses, tuition fees, scholarships, campuses, English language requirements, and admission criteria.

What AusUni Covers

🏛️ Universities

Detailed profiles of 40+ Australian universities including Group of Eight, regional universities, TAFEs, and private providers.

📚 Courses

Hundreds of undergraduate and postgraduate courses with tuition fees, duration, intake dates, and English requirements.

🏆 Scholarships

Comprehensive listings of scholarships available to international students, including government and university-specific awards.

📋 Visa Guides

Step-by-step guidance on Australian student visa applications, requirements, and post-study work options.

📊 Compare Tool

Side-by-side university comparison tool to help you evaluate your options based on fees, rankings, and courses.

✍️ Blog & Guides

In-depth articles and guides on student life, cost of living, English tests, part-time work, and more.

Our Mission

Our mission is simple: to give every international student — regardless of their background or resources — access to the same quality of information that students with expensive education consultants receive.

Studying abroad is one of the most important and expensive decisions a person can make. We believe that decision should be made with full, honest, and accurate information. Not with information that is incomplete, outdated, or filtered through the commercial interests of recruitment agencies.

AusUni is completely free to use. We do not charge students for access to any information on our platform. We are supported by advertising, which allows us to keep our service free for everyone.

Who We Are

AusUni is an independent platform run by a small team passionate about education and technology. We are not affiliated with any Australian university, government body, or education agency. We do not receive payment from universities to feature or promote their institutions.

Our team researches and updates university information regularly, drawing from official university websites, government education portals, and publicly available ranking data. We are committed to accuracy and update our data as often as possible to ensure students have access to the most current information available.

Our Data Sources

All information on AusUni is sourced from reputable, official sources including:

  • Official university websites and international student portals
  • Australian Government's Study in Australia portal (studyaustralia.gov.au)
  • CRICOS — Commonwealth Register of Institutions and Courses for Overseas Students
  • Australian Department of Home Affairs (visa information)
  • QS World University Rankings (qsrankings.com)
  • Times Higher Education World University Rankings
  • Australian Government's Quality Indicators for Learning and Teaching (QILT)
  • Australian Bureau of Statistics (cost of living data)

Important: Tuition fees, admission requirements, and scholarship details change regularly. Always verify information directly with the university before making any decisions.
