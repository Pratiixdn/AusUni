About AusUni

What is AusUni?

AusUni is an independent online guide dedicated to helping international students explore universities, courses, tuition fees, scholarships, and admission requirements across Australia. We believe that finding the right university should be transparent, accessible, and stress-free.

Our platform covers universities across all Australian states and territories — from world-ranked Group of Eight institutions like the University of Melbourne and UNSW Sydney, to emerging universities, TAFE institutes, and private providers.

Our Mission

To provide accurate, up-to-date, and freely accessible information about Australian higher education to international students worldwide. We aim to be the most comprehensive and honest guide available.

Data Sources

All data on AusUni is sourced from:

  • Official university websites
  • Australian Government's Study in Australia portal (studyaustralia.gov.au)
  • CRICOS (Commonwealth Register of Institutions and Courses for Overseas Students)
  • QS World University Rankings
  • Times Higher Education (THE) Rankings

Note: Information may change. Always verify directly with universities before making decisions.
