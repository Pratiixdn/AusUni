Scholarship Guide

How to find and win scholarships to study in Australia

Can International Students Get Scholarships in Australia?

Yes — and there are far more options than most students realise. Australia has hundreds of scholarships specifically designed for international students, ranging from small tuition fee discounts to fully funded awards that cover everything including airfare, living expenses, and health insurance.

The key is knowing where to look, understanding the eligibility requirements, and submitting strong applications well before the deadlines. Many students miss out on scholarships simply because they did not apply — either because they did not know the scholarship existed or because they assumed they would not be eligible.

This guide covers the main types of scholarships available, how to find them, and how to give yourself the best chance of winning one.

Types of Scholarships Available

🏛️ Australian Government Scholarships

The Australian Government funds several major scholarship programmes for international students. These are among the most prestigious and generous awards available.

  • Australia Awards Scholarships — Fully funded postgraduate scholarships for students from eligible developing countries including Nepal. Covers tuition, living expenses, airfare, and health insurance.
  • Destination Australia Scholarships — Up to AUD 15,000 per year for students who choose to study at regional Australian universities and campuses.
  • Research Training Program (RTP) — Covers tuition fees and provides a living stipend for PhD and Masters by Research students at Australian universities.
🎓 University Merit Scholarships

Almost every Australian university offers its own merit-based scholarships for high-achieving international students. These range from 10% to 100% tuition fee reductions.

  • Many are awarded automatically based on your academic results at the time of application — no separate application required
  • Some require a separate scholarship application with a personal statement
  • Deadlines vary — some close months before the course start date
  • Awards typically range from AUD 5,000 to full tuition waiver
🔬 Research Scholarships

For students pursuing PhD or Masters by Research programmes, there are dedicated research scholarships available through both the government and individual universities.

  • Research Training Program (RTP) stipends of approximately AUD 29,000 per year
  • University-specific research scholarships with additional top-up payments
  • Industry-funded research scholarships in partnership with companies
  • International Research Scholarships offered by individual faculties
🌏 Country-Specific Scholarships

Some scholarships are specifically designed for students from particular countries or regions.

  • Nepal: Australia Awards Scholarships, Aga Khan Foundation Scholarships
  • India: Various bilateral scholarship programmes
  • ASEAN countries: ASEAN-Australia Development Cooperation Program scholarships
  • Pacific Islands: Australia Pacific Training Coalition scholarships
🏢 External and Private Scholarships

Various organisations, foundations, and professional bodies offer scholarships to students in specific fields or from specific backgrounds.

  • Professional associations in engineering, medicine, law, and accounting
  • Community foundations and cultural organisations
  • Corporate scholarships from major Australian companies
  • Rotary International Peace Fellowships

How to Apply for Scholarships

Step 1 — Research Early

Start researching scholarships at least 12 months before your intended study start date. Many major scholarships — particularly Australia Awards — have application deadlines six to nine months before the academic year begins.

Step 2 — Check Eligibility Carefully

Read every eligibility requirement before investing time in an application. Key factors typically include nationality, academic results, field of study, level of study, and whether you have previously studied in Australia.

Step 3 — Gather Your Documents

Most scholarship applications require academic transcripts, a personal statement or essay, letters of recommendation, a CV, and proof of English language proficiency. Prepare these documents in advance so you are not rushing at the deadline.

Step 4 — Write a Strong Personal Statement

Your personal statement is often the most important part of a scholarship application. Be specific about your academic achievements, your career goals, and why you deserve this scholarship. Use our SOP Writing Guide for detailed advice.

Step 5 — Apply to Multiple Scholarships

Do not rely on a single scholarship application. Apply to the government scholarship, several university merit scholarships, and any external awards you are eligible for. The more applications you submit, the better your chances.

Step 6 — Submit Before the Deadline

Late applications are almost never accepted. Set reminders well in advance of each deadline and aim to submit at least one week early to allow time to fix any technical issues with the application portal.

Tips to Improve Your Chances

📈 Maintain a Strong GPA

Most merit scholarships require a minimum GPA. A strong academic record is your single most important asset in scholarship applications. Focus on your results from the beginning of your degree.

🌏 Consider Regional Study

Choosing a regional university or campus gives you access to the Destination Australia Scholarship worth up to AUD 15,000 per year — on top of any university merit scholarship you may receive.

📝 Tailor Each Application

A generic personal statement will not win scholarships. Customise your application for each award — address the specific criteria, values, and priorities of each scholarship programme.

👥 Get Strong References

Choose referees who know your work well and can speak specifically to your achievements and potential. Give them plenty of notice and provide them with information about the scholarship and your goals.

🎯 Focus on Development Impact

For government scholarships like Australia Awards, demonstrate clearly how your studies will benefit your home country. Selection committees prioritise candidates with a credible plan to apply their skills back home.

⏰ Apply Early Every Year

If you are unsuccessful, apply again. Many scholarship recipients were rejected in earlier rounds. Use the feedback from unsuccessful applications to strengthen your next attempt.
